- [thali-composition.html](thali-composition.html) — **2026-08-31.** Compose meal images by placing per-component cutouts (chole in a katori, roti on the rim) onto a thali at ingest time, instead of photographing every combination. Measured the case: 31 components can form 36,425 plates of 2-4; we photograph 24. Recommends one corpus of cutouts, reached via a two-class transition, proven on ONE hand-composited plate before any code. **PLAN ONLY, nothing implemented — four open questions, the first being whether a composed thali reads as food at all.**
