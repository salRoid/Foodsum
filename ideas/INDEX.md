# Foodsum — design explorations

Drawings and plans authored here (NOT pulled from Claude Design — that is
`Core/reference/`). Each line says what the file proposes and how it landed.

- [thali-composition.html](thali-composition.html) — **2026-08-31.** Compose meal images by placing per-component cutouts (chole in a katori, roti on the rim) onto a thali at ingest time, instead of photographing every combination. Measured the case: 31 components can form 36,425 plates of 2-4; we photograph 24. Recommends one corpus of cutouts, reached via a two-class transition, proven on ONE hand-composited plate before any code. **REJECTED 2026-08-31 by Sal, on sight of the first composed plate.** Step 1 ran: three cutouts generated, one thali composited (per-vessel cutout — circle mask for a katori, luminance key for a flatbread, since a white bowl on white cannot be keyed). It worked technically and Sal did not want it. Meal images stay PHOTOGRAPHS. Kept on file so the approach is not proposed again, and for the two findings that outlived it — the per-vessel cutout rule, and that Beacon's 1-bit converter renders an ordinary dish photo as a BLANK WHITE SQUARE.
